One network, local names, one backbone
The network
Rankine is the network and the contracting entity. Customers contract with Rankine, not with each firm.
The operating firms
Firms keep their local names and equity. AC Services DFW is Works No. 1, the first firm on the platform.
The backbone
Eight surfaces, and none owns data. Each is a scoped view onto one multi-tenant hierarchy, reached only through one gateway.
What follows measures the first rollout against that whole: how much is built, how much is switched on, and what turns the rest on.
